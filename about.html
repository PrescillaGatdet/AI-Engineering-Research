About Cantana

Building Canada's capacity for the AI era.

Cantana is a Canadian AI research and adoption organization. We study how AI is changing the country's work and industries, and help Canadians act on what we learn.

Why Cantana exists

Canada is an AI research superpower, but a slow adopter.

That gap is our reason for existing. Research alone does not create economic advantage; adoption does.

From an individual's career, to a small business's bottom line, to the country's competitiveness, we help Canadians adopt AI with confidence instead of watching the transition happen to them.

What we believe

Our principles

  • Evidence over hype

    Every recommendation traces back to research and reality, not whatever's trending this week.

  • Canadian by design

    Bilingual, privacy-respecting, and built around how Canadian businesses and rules actually work.

  • Outcomes, not slideware

    We measure success by what changes: careers advanced, tools live, value created.

  • One ecosystem

    Careers, businesses, organizations and institutions, connected by the same evidence base.

How we conduct research

Independent, task-level, Canadian.

We analyse the work people actually do, task by task, across Canadian industries. Our findings are not shaped by the tools or vendors we might later recommend, and we publish openly wherever we can, in English and French.

Our commitments
  • Privacy & compliance by default: data residency, PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 built into how we work.
  • Bilingual delivery: research and advisory work in English and French.
  • Industry depth: we go deep in your sector instead of one-size-fits-all advice.
  • Responsible adoption: governance, risk and trust are part of every engagement, not an afterthought.

The opportunity

Why now

The tools are good enough, the costs are low enough, and the gap between Canada's research strength and its adoption rate has never been wider. The businesses and professionals who move deliberately in the next few years will define their industries for the decade after. Cantana exists to make sure as many of them as possible are Canadian.
