What we do

Most AI commentary is global, generic and already out of date. Cantana's research is the opposite: focused on specific Canadian industries, grounded in primary data, and built to be referenced by the people making real decisions executives, boards, associations and government.

  • Workforce impact studies — which tasks in a role are automatable, augmentable, or resistant, with timelines and confidence levels.
  • Industry deep-dives — sector-by-sector analysis of where AI creates value and where it creates exposure.
  • Commissioned reports — custom studies for associations, funders and enterprises that need defensible numbers.
  • Policy & readiness briefs — concise, evidence-based input for strategy and regulation.

Method

Primary data, Canadian context

We combine task-level role analysis, practitioner interviews and live tool benchmarking — weighted for Canadian regulation, bilingual realities and regional economics. Every claim is sourced and reproducible.
