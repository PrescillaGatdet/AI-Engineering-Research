How we work

Our research standards

  • Independent

    Our findings are not shaped by the tools or vendors we might recommend.

  • Task-level

    We look at the work people actually do, not just job titles.

  • Canadian context

    Grounded in Canada's industries, regions, regulation and labour market.

  • Open & bilingual

    Published openly wherever possible, in English and French.
