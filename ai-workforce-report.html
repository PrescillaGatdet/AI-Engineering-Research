Why this report exists

Canada is one of the world's great AI research nations, yet adoption across our businesses lags. The conversation about jobs is dominated by global, sensational takes that don't reflect Canadian industries, regulation or regional economies. We built this report to replace fear and hype with something more useful: evidence, at the level of actual tasks.

How we analyze it

Rather than asking "will this job disappear," we break roles into their component tasks and assess each one:

  • Automatable — tasks AI can largely perform today, shifting the human role to oversight.
  • Augmentable — tasks where AI makes a person meaningfully faster or better.
  • Resilient — tasks that stay firmly human, for practical, physical or trust reasons.

The mix of those three across a role tells a far more honest story than any single headline number.

Most roles aren't "replaced" — they're recomposed. The winners are the people and businesses that re-mix toward the augmentable and resilient work fastest.

What you'll find inside

  3. The high-value work that opens up as routine work is automated.
  4. Practical recommendations for individuals, employers and policymakers.
