FAQ

Pricing questions

Why are some prices ranges?

Scope varies. A single-tool sprint sits at the low end; multi-team rollouts with training at the higher end. We confirm a fixed number after a short scoping call.

Can funding really cover part of it?

For many Canadian SMBs, yes — programs like BDC and regional innovation funding may offset a portion. We flag what you might qualify for; we don't guarantee approval.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes — an optional fractional AI lead keeps momentum after the first sprint. We'll quote it monthly.

What payment terms do you use?

Career audits are paid up front. Projects are typically split across milestones. Details are in your proposal.
